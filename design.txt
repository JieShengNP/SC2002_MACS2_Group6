Requirements
1. Total price sold (if fraction, display in 2dp)
2. End of turn 18: check if total price >= $45; no. of paid customer >= 4 ; average satisfaction of paid customers >= 60
  - All met: Victory
  - else: Defeat
3. No ordering/ eating if not seated.
4. UI: only collects the manager’s decisions and displays the state. Game rules belong in domain and control objects.
5. All mandatory features must work without network access.
6. An invalid choice must explain why it failed and let the manager choose again, without using up the staff task.
7. Revenue never includes unpaid or abandoned orders.
8. The customer who arrives on turn 16 cannot finish paying within 18 turns, so at most five customers can pay. This is intended, not a bug.

Setting:
1. 18 turns
2. 2 tables
3. 1 waiter (Wait; seat 1 queued customer at free table; take 1 order; serve 1 READY order); 1 chef (1 turn for salad and burger; 2 for pasta); 1 cashier (payment for READY_TO_PAY customers)
  - waiter acts before chef
    -  hence, dish finished this turn is served next turn the earliest.
  - dish: READY when last prep unit is done, SERVED when served.
  - customer: takes at least 2 turns to leave after being served (1 turn for eating, 1 turn after READY_TO_PAY)
    - cashier handles at most 1 READY_TO_PAY customer
    - after payment --> customer labelled as "PAID", frees the table
4. customer arrival: 1,4,7,10,13,16 (every 3n + 1 turns)
*5. Assume each customer only order 1 item (by seeded randomness)

Safisfaction calculation:
- Arrives with 100
- Deducts 8 points for each turn the customer is not served
- Leaves without paying if 0
  - cancels any unfinished order and free the table

START of each turn
Print: turn number; revenue; average satisfaction; waiting queue; occupied table; each customer's satisfaction; order status; staff tasks available. 

END of each turn
Print: served count; unhappy departures; revenue; avg satisfaction of paid cust (if no payment yet, show N/A); turns used

STAGE 1
(Basic operation)
Regular customers, menu, Waiter, Chef, Cashier, full turn sequence, both endings
Idea:
(Victory): 
  - 1 pasta or >=2 burger
    - each cycle (customer seated --> order taken --> served --> payment) takes at least 5 turns (waiter involved in 4 turns), hence, at most 4 paid customers. 
(Defeat):
  - all order salad (revenue <$45)

STAGE 2
(Customer behaviour)
VIP and Critic customers
VIP: 10% off 
Critic customers: Distinct order preference or reaction to service to be defined and documented. (defined directly in the object)
  - higher satisfaction, eg. leave when <50, instead of the usual =0
    - theoretical highest is 84 for 1 prep unit item; 76 for 2 prep unit

STAGE 3
(New role and menu policy)
Host, ComboMeal, Happy Hour
Host: Seats 1 customer per turn
ComboMeal: made of two existing menu items
  - Preparation units = the sum of its two components.
  - Price = 90% of combined price, rounded to the nearest cent. 
Happy Hour: A pricing policy the manager may switch on.
  - 1 activation/game, at the start of a turn, last for 2 turns (inclu. current turn)
    - Display remaining duration
  - 20% off (based on when order is taken)
  - +15 satisfaction if customer is still waiting for table or food at turn start (cap. 100)
  - Apply the VIP discount first, then Happy Hour. (equiv. 28% off)
  - The price is fixed when the order is taken.
  - It does not change orders already placed, and does not affect customers who have left.
